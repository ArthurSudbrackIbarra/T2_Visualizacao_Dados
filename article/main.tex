%% Abstract section.
\abstract{
  Brazil's Unified Health System (SUS) records every publicly funded hospital admission in the SIH/SUS dataset, including the patient's municipality of residence and the municipality where the admission took place.
  Together these fields describe how patients move across the country to be hospitalized, but the size of the data hides this pattern in ordinary tables and static maps.
  We present an interactive flow map that draws these movements as origin-destination arcs over a map of Brazil.
  The view is organized in three levels, country, state, and city, and supports filtering by medical specialty, year, and month, along with side panels that rank attracting and dependent municipalities and compare resident and incoming admissions across the year.
  Using more than 180 million admissions from 2008 to 2023, we show how the tool helps answer questions about patient export, care gaps, regional hubs, and seasonal demand.%
}
